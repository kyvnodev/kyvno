SYNTHRAID — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game SYNTHRAID ("the app") uses and why. The app is developed and published by an individual developer, Kyvno ("I", "me").

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, upgrades, ships, settings) is stored only on your device. I do not run any servers and do not collect, receive or sell this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch; others may appear between game sessions. To serve, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed views) and diagnostic data.

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. In-app purchases

If the app offers optional purchases (for example "Remove Ads"), payments are processed by Google Play; I never see your payment details. Purchase status may be verified through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data sharing and security

I do not sell or share your personal information. The third-party services listed above process data under their own privacy policies and transfer it using encryption (HTTPS).

6. Changes

I may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
